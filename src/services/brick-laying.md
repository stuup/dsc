DSC Complete Building Solutions are Brickwork Contractors offering a range of brickwork and blockwork solutions to main contractors and builders across the Wolverhampton and West Midlands area.

Providing labour, labour and mortar or full supply and fix bricklaying services our bricklayers can combine expertise in constructing traditional and modern brick bond patterns, panels, projections and arches with decorative brickwork such as arches, radials, doorways, angles, jambs and sills.

Whether a commercial or domestic new build project, extension, renovation or improvement our skilled tradesman offer qualified support to ensure your project is a success.
